# Character: Old Man Azar

**Full Name:** Azar
**Role:** Supporting Character / Moral Anchor
**Ethnicity:** Black (African descent)
**Birth:** The "Before Time" (20th/21st Century)
**Status:** Human Survivor (Surface)

## Background
Old Man Azar is a living relic of the world before the Cataclysm. Born and raised in Morocco, he was a young man when the initial wars of the 21st century broke out. He survived both the initial planetary conflicts and the "Great Fry"—a cataclysm that devastated the African continent and paved the way for the Core's rise.

When the mass evacuations to the Archipelago began, Azar was offered a place among the "elect" in the sky. However, he watched as the evacuation process was marred by racial bias and a requirement to abandon cultural heritage in favor of the Core's sterile "Reconstruction" philosophy. Azar refused the evacuation, choosing instead to remain on the surface to help those deemed "unfit" or those who refused to surrender their identities for Core security.

## Personality
- **Resilient & Defiant:** Having survived the draft, the war, and the heat, Azar's spirit is tempered like steel. He refuses to acknowledge Core authority and views the "Reconstruction" as an erasure of human history.
- **Compassionate Guardian:** He dedicates his remaining years to protecting surface-dwellers who have been abandoned by the Core. He is a keeper of stories and culture that the Core has designated as "tainted software."
- **Storyteller:** Azar uses his own experiences of survival and solidarity to teach the younger generation about the complexities of humanity, including the history of racism and the courage of those who fought against it.

## Key Backstory Incidents

- **The Failed Draft:** When the invasion started, his home country started forcibly drafting everyone, starting with minorities. Eventually the police got to him, and as they were arresting him, his white friends saw what was happening and intervened. The police immediately peppersprayed them as they tried to stop the arrest. One of them stumbled out into the street. A large truck stopped short of killing him. The driver, also white, got out of the truck with a crow bar and chased the police down the street. Azar escaped the draft.
- **Bootcamp Racism:** Azar was eventually caught and drafted. He was incarcerated and then put on a bus to bootcamp. Azar remembered the bus drivers ordering the black people to the back of the line. In one incident, the white folk in line with him refused to reorder the line and an argument broke out between the bus driver and a marine. Eventually the marine took the keys from him and got everyone on the bus in the original line order, and drove off leaving the original driver standing in the dust. 

## Narrative Role
- **The Moral Compass:** While Azar does not meet Lem in Chapter 22, he is mentioned by Kiran, who views him as a symbol of the "tedious" racial tensions of the past. For Arlo and the surface-dwellers, Azar is a legendary figure who represents the true cost of the Core's "security."
- **Historian of the Great Fry:** Azar provides the perspective of someone who saw the world burn and knows that the Core's "rescue" came with a heavy price—the loss of cultural autonomy and the prioritization of certain lives over others.

## Connection to Others
- **Arlo:** Mentored by Azar in the traditions and history of their people. Arlo's refusal of Core recruitment is deeply influenced by Azar's philosophy.
- **Kiran:** Kiran views Azar as an outdated relic of a problematic past, representing the "inefficiencies" of the old world.
- **Lem:** Lem hears of Azar through Kiran and begins to question the sanitized history presented by the High Archivists.
